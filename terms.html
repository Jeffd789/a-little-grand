Scope and operator

A Little Grand is a channel featuring digitally rendered piano performances and animated studio scenes. A Little Grand Publisher is its local, operator-only application for preparing and publishing approved releases to the owner's accounts. It is not a public account-management service, and this website does not let visitors connect social accounts.

The operator can be contacted at pianoautomationvids@gmail.com.

Authorization and use of the publishing app

Only the owner or a person expressly authorized by the owner may operate the application. Before authorizing a connection, the operator must review and accept these terms and the Privacy Policy, verify the destination account, and authorize the intended uploads and schedule. Permission to connect a provider does not grant rights to use someone else's music or recordings.

The owner is responsible for reviewing release content, credits, permissions, audience settings, destination accounts, and scheduling instructions. The application may hold a release when verification, authorization, supported media specifications, or provider status is uncertain.

Provider availability, API access, quotas, reviews, and credentials can change. No uninterrupted publishing, permanent authorization, exact delivery time, audience reach, or monetization outcome is promised. A platform may require the owner to sign in again or complete a review.

Content and music rights

The website and channel identity belong to A Little Grand or their respective rights holders. Compositions, score editions, samples, recordings, and visual assets may have different rights and license terms. Credits with a release describe its sources; they do not grant viewers a blanket license to reuse all channel material. Contact the owner about a specific use or concern.

The performances and studio scenes are digitally rendered. They should not be presented as footage of a live human pianist or as an endorsement by a composer, instrument maker, or platform.

Stopping the app and managing published content

The owner can disable future publishing and revoke provider access. Stopping the local app does not automatically cancel a post already accepted or scheduled by a provider. The owner must review those queues and manage existing posts in the relevant platform. The Privacy Policy explains revocation and requests to remove local account data.

The site and local publishing tool may change as the channel develops; material changes to these terms will be posted with an updated date.
