Who uses the publishing app

A Little Grand Publisher is an operator-only application run on the channel owner's Windows computer. It checks the owner's configured destinations, uploads or schedules approved videos, and checks their publishing status. It is not a public service for connecting other people's social accounts.

The public website has no account creation or OAuth sign-in. Viewers do not authorize the publishing app by visiting this site. The operator authorizes the relevant providers separately and controls when publishing is enabled.

The operator and privacy contact is A Little Grand, reachable at pianoautomationvids@gmail.com.

How information is used and shared

Account data is used to authenticate the owner, confirm the right destination, publish the owner's content, monitor connection health, and reconcile publishing results. It is not sold, used to target advertising, supplied to data brokers, or used to train AI models.

Finished videos and their titles, captions, scheduling instructions, and related publication metadata are sent to the selected platform, or to Buffer for the connected destination. Buffer and each destination process that information under their own policies. The app does not transfer Google authorization credentials or Google account data to Buffer or Meta; a finished video prepared by the owner may independently be published to several platforms.

When the owner explicitly configures public media hosting, the app uploads finished video files to the configured host, such as Cloudflare R2, and supplies their public HTTPS URLs to the publishing provider. Those video URLs are publicly accessible. Credentials, local rights documents, and verification reports are not uploaded as public media by this workflow.

Google API Limited Use. A Little Grand Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is limited to the owner-authorized publishing and account-verification functions described here, consistent with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is not used for unrelated profiling, advertising, or resale.

The owner may inspect local operational records to maintain the application. The app does not provide a public analytics dashboard or access to a third party's connected accounts. Google and YouTube also process information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage, security, and retention

Credentials are stored locally using Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI), bound to the Windows user and machine. This includes refresh tokens, configured provider secrets, and saved resumable-upload session URLs. The application does not store the owner's Google password. Access tokens are used to make authorized API requests.

Account IDs, owner email, configuration, publishing queue records, remote post/video IDs and links, connection-health results, and release evidence may be stored on the owner's computer. Operational records are not all encrypted by DPAPI; their protection also depends on Windows account access and the owner's local storage and backup practices. Credential values and raw provider error bodies are excluded from the app's normal logs.

The app refreshes connection information when health checks run. Its YouTube maintenance removes stored API publishing records when their last confirmed API observation is 29 days old, and removes stale YouTube information from local operational reports. This maintenance runs during health, publishing, and receipt checks; it only runs while the local application is operating. The owner is responsible for regular maintenance and handling requests promptly, including when the publishing host has been offline. Other providers’ local operational records remain until the owner removes them. Local source videos, captions, licensing evidence, and owner-entered configuration are separate from information received from a provider.

Your control, revocation, and removal

The operator can disable publishing, revoke a provider connection, and remove its local credentials and associated account records. The local YouTube disconnect workflow previews the affected data before the owner explicitly applies it. Applying disconnect blocks further YouTube publishing, attempts to revoke the Google authorization, and removes local YouTube credentials, saved upload sessions, API publishing receipts, and YouTube entries in operational reports. Minimal local video hashes remain to prevent duplicate uploads; these hashes contain no YouTube video IDs or account data. If remote revocation cannot be confirmed, the app tells the owner to revoke access in Google settings. This workflow does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube.

  • Google and YouTube: revoke A Little Grand Publisher's access in Google's account permissions settings. Manage or delete uploaded videos separately in YouTube Studio.
  • Meta: remove the app or its permissions in the relevant Facebook/Instagram account or business integration settings.
  • Buffer: disconnect the social channel or revoke the publishing API key in the owner's Buffer settings. Review any already queued posts in Buffer.

Google revocation may apply to all authorized scopes and clients within the same Google project. The publisher uses a dedicated project for this channel. A confirmed invalid Google refresh grant also triggers local YouTube cleanup when the running app detects it. Revocation cannot be detected while the host is offline.

For access, correction, or deletion of app-held account data, contact pianoautomationvids@gmail.com. The owner handles these requests and runs the local cleanup workflow as soon as possible. The owner is responsible for completing requested deletion of stored YouTube API data within seven calendar days, including manually running cleanup if the normal publishing host is offline. This application is not offered to outside account holders and has no public self-service deletion portal. Removing local data does not delete a provider's copies, published posts, or public hosted video objects; those must be managed at the relevant service.

Visitors to this website

This static site has no audience sign-up, contact form, analytics scripts, advertising pixels, embedded videos, or tracking cookies added by A Little Grand. Links lead to external platforms only when you choose to follow them. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and protect the site.

If you email us, the message and contact information are handled through our email provider so the owner can respond. Do not send passwords, API tokens, or other credentials by email.
